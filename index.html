About Environmental Clearance Process

Environmental clearance is a mandatory requirement for projects that may impact the environment. Our portal digitizes the entire workflow, making it transparent, efficient, and accessible.

🌳

Environmental Impact Assessment

Comprehensive evaluation of potential environmental impacts before project approval. Our system ensures thorough assessment of ecological, social, and environmental factors.

💻

Digital Workflow Management

End-to-end digitization of the clearance process from application submission to final approval. Track your application status in real-time with complete transparency.

📋

Document Verification

Automated and expert-led verification of submitted documents. Instant notifications for deficiencies with clear guidelines for corrections.

👥

Expert Committee Review

Scheduled expert panel meetings with transparent decision-making processes. Minutes of meetings (MoM) are documented and accessible to stakeholders.

🔔

Real-time Notifications

Automated alerts for application status updates, meeting schedules, and required actions. Stay informed at every step of the process.

🛡️

Compliance Monitoring

Post-approval monitoring and compliance tracking to ensure environmental conditions are met throughout the project lifecycle.
